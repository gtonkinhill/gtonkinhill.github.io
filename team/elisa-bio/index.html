Elisa Sosa

Elisa is a visiting PhD student from the University of Zurich, Switzerland, working in Prof. Adrian Egli’s lab at the Institute of Medical Microbiology. Her project investigates the evolution of antimicrobial resistance, focusing on longitudinal, within-host evolution and E. coli strain dynamics. Elisa joined the Tonkin-Hill lab to leverage the team’s tools and expertise to analyse her genomic data and develop a scalable pipeline. Her 6-month research stay is supported by the SNF Mobility grant from the Swiss National Science Foundation and a SEMP stipend by the University of Zurich.
